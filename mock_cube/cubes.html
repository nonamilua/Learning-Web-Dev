MY SPEEDCUBES ^.^


These are some of the good speedcubes I own and use for practicing u.u I take very good care of them.

  • The Moyu RS3M 2020!! A classic modern speedcube. This was my first main and I still use it from time to time. I like how it's slower and more controllable compared to other more recent cubes I own. The corner cutting is amazing and it makes lower-pitched noises, which I (and the people around me (ᵕ—ᴗ—)) really appreciate.

    RS3M 2020

  • X-Man Tornado V3. This is a bit of a weird one. It's very stable and fast, but I do feel my movements are a bit restricted by the extremely strong magnets. It's not as smooth as I'd like it to be. And the color contrast fucking sucks!! ( ` ᴖ ´ ) I don't know how they managed to get that wrong...It is an extremely silent cube tho, that's probably it's best feature.

    Tornado V3

  • This is the...(brace yourself) Moyu RS3M V5 Maglev Ball-Core UV!! Basically a modern 'premium' remake of the classic RS3M 2020. It's faster and turns more effortlessly, the auto-alignment really makes your life easier for flick turns (๑>◡<๑) This is my current main.

    RS3M V5
